# UrbanNest Properties Product Catalog

**Important Notice:** UrbanNest Properties is a fictional demonstration company created for Niteesh AI Growth Labs and must never be represented as an active commercial entity.

### 1. UrbanNest Prime Residences
- **Price Range:** ₹1.20 Cr – ₹2.50 Cr
- **Markets:** Bandra West, Worli, Powai (Mumbai)
- **Typologies:** 3 BHK & 4 BHK Sky Mansions
- **Key Features:** Panoramic Arabian sea views, 11-foot clear floor-to-ceiling heights, Italian marble flooring, private keycard elevators, 82% usable carpet efficiency.

- **Price Range:** ₹75 Lakh – ₹1.35 Cr

- **Typologies:** 1.5 BHK, 2 BHK, 3 BHK Smart Homes
- **Key Features:** High-speed fiber connected clubhouse, EV car charging infrastructure, co-working breakout pods, 80% open landscaped greens.

### 3. UrbanNest Investor Units
- **Price Range:** ₹90 Lakh – ₹1.80 Cr
- **Markets:** Hinjawadi Phase 1, Kharadi (Pune), Airoli (Navi Mumbai)
- **Typologies:** Managed Studio Suites & 1 BHK High-Yield Suites
- **Key Features:** Contractual corporate leasing tie-up with IT multinationals, guaranteed 6.2% gross rental yield for the first 3 years, end-to-end tenant management desk.
