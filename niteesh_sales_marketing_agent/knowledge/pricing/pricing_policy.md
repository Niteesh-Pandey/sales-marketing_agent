# UrbanNest Pricing & Payment Policy

### Standard Construction-Linked Plan (CLP)
- 10% Initial Booking Application Token
- 10% Execution & Registration of Agreement (within 30 days)
- 15% Completion of Plinth Level
- 10% Each for 4th, 8th, 12th, and 16th Slabs
- 15% Internal Plaster and Tile Finishes
- 10% Intimation of Handover & Key Possession

### 10:90 Bank Subvention Special Scheme
Available on UrbanNest Prime Residences:
- Buyer pays 10% now.
- 90% funded via partner bank pre-approvals (HDFC, SBI, ICICI).
- No Pre-EMI interest until possession or 24 months.

### Guaranteed 6.2% Rental Yield Payout
- Quarterly rental dividends credited directly into investor bank accounts for 36 months on UrbanNest Investor Units.
